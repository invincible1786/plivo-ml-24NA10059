End-of-Turn Detection

Deep Acoustic Signal Processing & Machine Learning Solution

The Strategy

We built a zero-lookahead, causal feature extraction pipeline that models prosody and conversation state directly from raw audio segments preceding pause_start.

By extracting 24 structured signal features, our model characterizes the voice trailing off at the end of turns compared to breath pauses and hesitations within active turns:

  • Zero-Padding Lookahead Prevention: Set `center=False` in all `librosa` feature extraction calls.
  • Causal Pause End Detector: Analyzes energy on past completed pauses to track durations without lookahead.
  • Fricative vs. Breath discriminator: Computes the flatness to RMS energy ratio to detect consonants.
  • State & MFCC Features: Sequences speech duration, pause index, and first 6 MFCCs.
